The lab has what a board needs from idea to test:

- Measurement, soldering and rework equipment.
- A CNC router.
- A pick and place machine.
- A vapour reflow oven.
- A stencil printer.
- A spot welder.
- More than 1300 electronic parts, collected over the years.

The projects built here are on [the work page](@/work.md).
