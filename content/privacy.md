## Who we are

This site is run by VHRD.TECH AS, Asker, Norway. Questions about your data go to {{ <vh.mail to="contact" subject="Privacy" /> }}.

## What the site keeps

- **No cookies, no analytics scripts, nothing from other sites.** Pages, fonts and pictures all come from this server.
- **Your theme choice**, if you switch to the light theme, stays in your own browser. It never reaches us.
- **Access logs.** Like any web server, ours writes down each request: your IP address, the time, the page asked for and what your browser says it is. We keep them to run the site and to find and stop abuse, and delete them after about 15 days.
- **Visitor counts.** Once a day the server counts from those logs how often each page was viewed, from which countries and from which sites people came. Known bots are left out, and each IP address is shortened before anything is counted, so the counts hold totals only: no IP address, no cookie, no script on the pages, nothing that singles out one visitor. The country comes from a free database (DB-IP) kept on the server; nothing is sent to anyone else. We use the counts to judge which pages and products people are interested in, nobody else sees them, and we keep them for 24 months.

## The signup list

When you leave your address in a "Get notified" form for [Donguru](@/donguru.md), we send it one mail with a button to confirm it. The address goes on the list only when you press that button: you give your consent there, not by sending the form. We keep:

- your e-mail address;
- the list you signed up for;
- the time you signed up;
- which version of the text next to the form you saw (this one is 2026-11-06);
- the time you confirmed;
- how many mails we sent to it, and when the last one went out.

**Why:** to send you one mail when the Donguru campaign opens. We use the address for nothing else, and we do not give or sell it to anyone. You can take your consent back at any time.

**Not confirmed:** an address nobody confirms is deleted after 7 days.

**Removing it:** every mail from us has a link that removes you. Its button deletes the address from the list at once.

**Where:** in a plain file on the web server we rent in Lithuania, outside the site's public folders. The mails go out through our own mail server, mail.vhrd.tech.

**How long:** until the campaign has opened and the mail has gone out, then we delete the list within three months.

**To stop abuse,** the server remembers how many sign-ups and how many presses of the buttons in our mails came from your IP address in the last hour, and how many sign-ups came from its network in the last day. That count stays in memory only and is never written down.

## Your rights

You can ask what we keep about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. To be removed from the list, use the link in any of our mails, reply to it or write to {{ <vh.mail to="donguru" subject="Remove me" /> }}. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.
