The old shop on vhrd.tech is closed, and a new one is on its way. Until it opens, boards are ordered by mail.

## How to order

- Write to {{ <vh.mail to="contact" subject="Order" /> }}.
- Say which board you want (FlexyRJ45, or another one) and how many.
- Give the country it ships to.
- The reply gives the price, shipping and how to pay.
