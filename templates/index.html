Donguru sits between your computer and a board with a USB-C port. One cable carries USB, a serial console and power you can switch and measure.

  1. Hub and serial in one plug

    A USB hub and a UART on the same cable. TX and RX are flipped for you.

  2. Power under control

    Electronic switches on VBUS and the USB data lines, a power meter, and a mechanical switch for the boot mode.

  3. Scriptable

    A fast CLI, donguru or dg, with a Rust driver and Python behind it.

For development, debugging and production flashing of devices with a USB-C port.
