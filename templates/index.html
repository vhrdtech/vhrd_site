Donguru sits between your computer and a device with a USB-C port. One cable carries USB, the device's sideband pins and power you can switch and measure.

  1. Hub and sideband pins in one plug

    A USB 2.0 Hi-Speed hub, and UART, I2C, SWD, FDCAN, GPIO and ADC on the plug's SBU pins. The cable orientation is a setting: a flipped cable is one command, not re-plugging.

  2. Power under control

    A VBUS switch up to 20 V and 5 A, off at power-up, a power meter, and a switch on the device's USB data lines. Guards in the firmware cut VBUS at a voltage, current or power limit.

  3. Scriptable

    A CLI, dg, with a Rust library and a Python wheel behind it, and a CMSIS-DAP debug probe for probe-rs, OpenOCD and pyOCD.

For bring-up, debugging, flashing and production test of devices with a USB-C port.
